# Scoring rubric (1-10)

Anchors for every category: 3 = actively hurts the video, 5 = forgettable, 7 = good enough to post, 9 = would share it.

- **hook** - seconds 0-3. 9: you physically can't swipe (a question, motion, a pattern break in frame 1). 5: pleasant but nothing makes you stay. 3: a slow fade, a logo, or silence first.
- **pacing** - cut rhythm and density. 9: every beat is new, nothing lingers. 5: some shots outstay their welcome. 3: long static stretches, slow gaps between lines.
- **retention** - would you be watching at 50% and at the end? Are there mid-video escalations or reveals pulling you forward?
- **visuals** - craft, appeal, variety, readability of on-screen text at phone size.
- **sound** - voice (human vs. robotic, energy), music (present? fits? energy?), SFX, mix. No music on a reel usually caps this at 5.
- **story** - clear problem, tension, payoff; a twist that lands; one idea, not five.
- **cta** - clear ask, readable handle/URL held long enough, earned by what came before.

Score what is there, not what could be. Half points are allowed.
